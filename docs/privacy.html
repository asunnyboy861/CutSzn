Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

CutSzn is built local-first. Your weight, urine specific gravity (USG), meals, and cut plans stay on your device unless you explicitly export them or share them with a coach.

1. Data we store on your device

CutSzn uses Apple's SwiftData framework to store the following locally:

  • Body weight entries and weigh-in timestamps
  • Cut plans, target weights, weigh-in dates
  • USG test-strip values
  • Meal logs and nutrition estimates
  • Protocol check-ins and refuel progress
  • App preferences such as units and reminder settings

This data is not transmitted to any CutSzn server. If you enable iCloud, Apple may sync it through your private iCloud database under Apple's terms.

2. Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, CutSzn reads your latest body mass from HealthKit and can write your weigh-ins back. HealthKit data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never sent to a CutSzn server. You can revoke permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

3. AI features

AI is optional. The glide path, protocol, safety verdict, and refuel plan are deterministic and local.

  • Apple Intelligence: on supported devices, prompts are processed on-device or through Apple Private Cloud Compute under Apple's privacy terms.
  • Your own API key: when you configure a provider, your prompt and (for meal scans) the photo are sent directly to the provider you chose, under that provider's privacy policy. CutSzn does not receive them.
  • CutSzn Cloud quota: if you subscribe and use our cloud quota, prompts are forwarded through a proxy to our AI provider solely to produce your answer. We do not store chat content or meal photos after the request completes.

API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. They are never included in analytics, logs, or our servers.

4. Feedback you send us

If you use Contact Support, we receive your name, email, subject, message, and the app name. We use it only to respond to you. We do not add you to marketing lists.

5. Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple. CutSzn receives entitlement status, not your payment details. Apple's privacy policy governs the transaction.

6. Analytics and tracking

CutSzn does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. The app's privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types.

7. Sharing with a coach

Team sharing is initiated by you and uses a CloudKit CKShare link with read-only permission. You can revoke the link at any time by removing the share in iCloud. Coaches cannot edit your data.

8. Children

CutSzn is rated 12+ and is intended for athletes old enough to follow a safety protocol. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require an account.

9. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete the app or clear its data. To delete everything, remove the app from your device. Feedback emails can be deleted on request.
