How do I log my morning weight?

Open Today and tap Weigh in. The first fasted weight of the day is the value we use; later entries are marked as reference. If you allow Apple Health, tap "Import weight from Apple Health" during onboarding and future weigh-ins can sync there.

How do I log a USG test strip?

Open Plan → USG compliance → Log. Enter the strip value between 1.005 and 1.035. NFHS limit is 1.025, NCAA limit is 1.020. Anything above 1.030 triggers a forced hydration warning.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open the iOS Settings app → tap your Apple ID → Subscriptions → select CutSzn → Cancel Subscription. Canceling stops the next renewal; access continues until the current period ends.

Single-Cut Pass is a 30-day non-renewing purchase. It never charges again automatically.

How do I restore purchases?

Open CutSzn → Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that bought the plan.

Why does AI say "offline coach"?

CutSzn tries Apple Intelligence on-device first, then any API key you configured, then your cloud quota, then a deterministic offline answer. The core glide path, protocol, safety verdict, and refuel plan never need a network connection.

How do I add my own API key?

Settings → AI → Advanced: Configure Custom API. Choose a provider or enter any OpenAI-compatible base URL, model name, and key. Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, never in our servers. Meal scanning needs a vision-capable model.

How do I share my dashboard with a coach?

Team tab → Create coach link. This uses iCloud and requires you to be signed in. The coach gets a read-only view of your status and USG report and cannot edit your data.

Where is my data stored?

Weight, USG, meals, and plans are stored on your device with SwiftData. Nothing is uploaded to a CutSzn server. See our Privacy Policy.

Is CutSzn medical advice?

No. CutSzn is training guidance. Cutting weight carries real risks, and serious dehydration can be fatal. If you feel dizzy, confused, or stop sweating, stop and seek help. Red verdicts are intentionally non-overridable, including with a paid plan.
